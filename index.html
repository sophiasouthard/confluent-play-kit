Confluent Play Kit

Pair Confluent with the vendors your customers already run

Overview

Why this play works, and what each kit contains.

One simple play, repeated for each vendor your customers already run.

Each kit gives sellers one trigger, one question, three benefits, one proof point and a short email for a vendor your customers already run: Databricks, MongoDB, Snowflake, Splunk and Datadog. Databricks and MongoDB lead, based on partner feedback about where the joint motion is strongest. The Upsell Opportunities tab adds two plays for customers who already run Kafka but do not yet use Confluent.

Why now

Confluent is now part of IBM

IBM completed its acquisition of Confluent (about $11B enterprise value). That puts Confluent inside the IBM portfolio. [S1]

Confluent is building for resellers

Confluent launched "Sell With Confluent" for solution providers: automated quotes, instant approvals and co-marketing funds. Confirm eligibility [S2]

Play Library

One kit per pairing: Brief, Email, Talk Track, Personas, Account Checklist. Databricks and MongoDB are listed first as the lead plays.

Upsell Opportunities

Customers who already run Kafka but do not yet use Confluent. Same kit format as the Play Library: Brief, Email, Talk Track, Personas, Account Checklist.

Check before you reach out These plays only fit accounts that are not already Confluent customers. Ask the Confluent or IBM account team to confirm that before sending anything. All cost figures in these kits are Confluent's own claims and are labeled that way.

Amazon MSK

Teams already on AWS who chose a hosted Kafka service. The pitch is cost, less cluster work and a migration path that does not need a big-bang cutover. [S17] [S19]

Self-managed Kafka

Teams running open-source Kafka on their own infrastructure. The pitch is the people and infrastructure cost of running it, plus the managed services around it. [S18]

How to find them

Hypothesis Ask the Confluent or IBM team for an installed-base list and a Kafka-user list, then remove existing Confluent customers. Check your own CRM notes for MSK or Kafka mentions.
